This manual documents Universal Core's own pages — every generated list,
form, import, export, and report screen links here through its own "?"
button. You can also open it directly at any time by browsing to
`/help`.

## Finding a topic

The left pane lists every topic you have access to, grouped by module.
Type into the search box above the list to narrow it down live as you
type — no need to press a button or reload the page.

Topics marked **Admin** cover admin-facing behavior. They are not
hidden from anyone; the badge just flags that the topic describes
something an ordinary user typically won't configure themselves.

## Reading a topic

Select any topic in the left pane to open it on the right. A topic
covering an entity type you don't currently have read access to simply
never appears in the list or in search results — there is nothing to
open for it.

![The help manual's own two-pane layout: a searchable topic tree on the left, the selected topic's content on the right](/help/assets/help/en.jpg)

## Getting here from anywhere else

Every other page's own "?" button (next to its title) opens this same
manual, already scrolled to that page's own topic — the fastest way in
is usually from whatever page you actually have a question about, not
this page itself.
