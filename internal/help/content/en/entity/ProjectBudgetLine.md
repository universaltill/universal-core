A Project Budget Line is one planned cost category for a Project — how
much you expect to spend on labour, materials, travel, equipment, or
other costs, before any of it happens.

## When to use it

Add a Budget Line for each cost category you want to plan for on a
project, so the project's budget breaks down into something you can
compare category by category rather than one lump figure.

## Adding a budget line

1. On the **Project** form's Budget Lines section, add a row (or open
   **Budget Line** directly and choose **New**).
2. Choose the **Project**.
3. Choose a **Category** — Labour, Materials, Travel, Equipment, or
   Other. It defaults to Labour.
4. Enter the **Planned Amount**.
5. Save.

## Rules to know

- Project, Category, and Planned Amount are required. Planned Amount
  defaults to 0.
- Category is a fixed list, not free text, so the categories stay
  consistent enough to compare and group across a project's budget
  lines.
- A Budget Line is planning only — it records what you expect to spend,
  in the project's own currency. It does not itself compute or show
  what has actually been spent against it.

## What it connects to

A Project Budget Line belongs to a **Project**. It has no connection to
Time Entries or any other record of actual cost — the two are tracked
separately in this product today.
